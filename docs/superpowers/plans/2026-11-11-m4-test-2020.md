# Milestone 4: Test (2020) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Test whether companies that filed their annual report after COVID-19 arrived rewrote their risk sections or added to them, using companies that filed just before as the comparison and earlier years to take out the standing differences between early and late filers.

**Architecture:** One analysis table (a row per filing, with its filing date, its exposure, and milestone 2's shares), then three estimators on it: a difference-in-differences with company and year fixed effects, an event study by filing week, and the same difference-in-differences run on years with no shock. Standard errors are clustered by company. A descriptive section covers the following year, where the pandemic and the SEC's new rules can't be separated.

**Tech Stack:** pandas; pyfixest (fixed-effects regression with clustered errors).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-copying-rework-design.md` (Layer 3). Inputs: `data/interim/filings.parquet` (filing dates, milestone 1), `data/interim/filing_metrics.parquet` and `data/interim/labels/` (milestone 2), `data/interim/chains.parquet` (milestone 3).

## Measured before planning (2026-11-11)

- Paired filings with a fiscal year ending in December and a single matching 10-K: about 2,450 a year for fiscal 2015 to 2019.
- Share filed on or after 11 March of the following year: 40.7%, 39.8%, 38.1%, 39.7% for fiscal 2015–2018 and 38.7% for fiscal 2019. The split is stable.
- Filing time is persistent: a company that filed late one year files late the next 79–86% of the time; one that filed early files late about 10% of the time.
- In 2020, 5.7% filed after 1 May, against about 2% in earlier years: the SEC's COVID extension moved some filers later. Who took it is unlikely to be random, which is why Task 2 also assigns exposure by the *previous* year's filing date.

## Global Constraints (all fixed before any outcome is looked at)

- **Sample:** filings for fiscal 2016–2019 that have a previous-year filing, a fiscal year ending in December in both years, and `match_status == "unique"`.
- **Exposure:** `late` = filed on or after 11 March of the year after the fiscal year (2020-03-11 is the day the WHO declared a pandemic). The same calendar rule defines `late` in the comparison years.
- **Event year:** fiscal 2019. **Comparison years:** fiscal 2016, 2017, 2018.
- **Outcomes:** `share_new`, `share_removed`, `share_near` (edited in place), `growth` (log of this year's sentence count over last year's), and `share_new_ex_covid` (new sentences that don't mention COVID, as a share of all sentences).
- **Reading the result:** *append* = new sentences rise and removals don't; *rewrite* = both rise. A rise confined to sentences that mention COVID, with `share_new_ex_covid` flat, is appending in its plainest form.
- **Inference:** standard errors clustered by company; 95% intervals; no stars.
- COVID mention: the sentence matches `covid|coronavirus|sars-cov|2019-ncov`, case-insensitive. ("Pandemic" alone is not counted: filings used it for influenza years before.)
- Every number in `docs/test-2020.md` comes from `results/test2020.json`.

## Review Focus

1. A leap year: 11 March 2020 is one day later in the year than 11 March 2019; exposure is set from calendar dates, not day counts. (Task 1)
2. A company that changed its fiscal year end: out of the main sample, in the robustness sample. (Task 1)
3. A company with only one year in the sample: it adds nothing to a fixed-effects estimate and must not break it. (Task 2)
4. An event-study week with no event-year filings: it is merged into its neighbor rather than estimated from nothing. (Task 3)
5. A placebo year must not include fiscal 2019 in its comparison group. (Task 4)

### Task 2: Difference-in-differences

**Interfaces:**
- `did(table, outcome, event_year=2019, comparison=(2016, 2017, 2018), exposure="late") -> dict`: `outcome ~ exposure + exposure × event | company + year`, clustered by company. Returns the estimate, standard error, interval, p-value, filings, companies, and the four cell means (early/late by comparison/event).
- Robustness, each a call with one thing changed: `exposure="late_usual"`; no company fixed effects; `multiple` rows added; companies with a changed fiscal year end added; filers within three weeks either side of the cutoff only.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests.** On a simulated panel with a true effect of 0.03 for late filers in the event year and none otherwise, the interval contains 0.03 and excludes 0; with no effect, the interval contains 0; a company present in one year only doesn't change the estimate by more than rounding; the cell means match a direct group-by.
- [ ] **Step 2–5:** run, implement, run, commit "Estimate the difference-in-differences".

### Task 3: Event study by filing week

**Interfaces:**
- `event_study(table, outcome, event_year=2019, comparison=(2016, 2017, 2018), reference=-2) -> list[dict]`: weeks relative to the cutoff in bins `≤ -4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3 to 6, 7 and later`; `outcome ~ bin + bin × event | company + year`; one estimate with interval per bin, relative to the reference week (the busiest week before the cutoff), plus each bin's count of event-year filings.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests.** With an effect only in weeks 0 and later, the estimates before week 0 have intervals containing 0 and those after contain the effect; the reference bin is reported as zero with no interval; a bin with fewer than 20 event-year filings is merged into its neighbor.
- [ ] **Step 2–5:** run, implement, run, commit "Add the event study by filing week".

### Task 4: Placebos, the COVID check, and the year after

**Interfaces:**
- `placebos(table, outcome) -> list[dict]`: `did` with fiscal 2017 as the event year (comparison 2016 and 2018) and with fiscal 2018 (comparison 2016 and 2017); fiscal 2019 is in neither.
- `covid_by_month(table) -> list[dict]`: for fiscal 2018, 2019 and 2020, by month filed: filings, share mentioning COVID, median `share_covid`.
- `year_after(cfg) -> dict`: for fiscal 2020 December filers, split at 2020-11-09 where both sides have filings: the five outcomes' medians; the share of filings with a heading containing "summary" and "risk"; the share with a "general risk factors" heading; the share of sentences added in fiscal 2019 that are still there in fiscal 2020, for COVID sentences and others, next to the same share for sentences added in fiscal 2016–2018.

- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests.** `placebos` never passes a 2019 row to the estimator; heading detection finds "Summary of Risk Factors" and "Risk Factor Summary" and not "Summary of Significant Accounting Policies"; the survival share matches a hand count on a fixture.
- [ ] **Step 2–5:** run, implement, run, commit "Add placebos, the COVID check and the year after".
